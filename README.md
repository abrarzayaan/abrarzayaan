## 👋 About Me

Most of start with *"Which framework should we use?"*  
I start with: **"Where is this business losing time, money, or trust, and which part of the system causes it?"**

Slow reports, a database that struggles on busy days, stock that goes negative, numbers that don't match between dashboards, a system that works for 10 vendors but breaks at 100. These are **architecture problems wearing a bug costume**, and they are what I fix.

## 🧠 How I Think

1. 🎯 **Business first, technology second.** Every technical decision traces back to a business outcome.
2. ⚡ **Correctness before speed, speed before cleverness.**
3. 🛡️ **Design for failure.** Assume a component will break and decide now what happens next.
4. 📏 **Measure everything.** No before/after number means the job isn't finished.
5. 🧩 **Simple until proven otherwise.** Distribution and complexity only when the problem demands it.

## 🌐 Where I'm Heading: Distributed Systems

My direction is deliberate: **distributed systems and event-driven architecture.**

- 🛡️ **Resilience:** one failure should not stop sales
- 📈 **Scale on demand:** handle the spike without paying for idle infrastructure
- 🧩 **Independent growth:** services evolve without breaking each other
- 🔒 **Trustworthy data across services:** consistency that keeps money and stock numbers correct

**Currently deepening:** message brokers & event streaming · service decomposition · replication & partitioning · consistency models · observability.

> I'll tell you honestly when your business does **not** need distribution yet. The best architecture is the simplest one that survives your next stage of growth.
